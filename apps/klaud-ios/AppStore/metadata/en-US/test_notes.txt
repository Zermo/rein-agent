klaʊdbot 1.0.0 — live field console (WKWebView of openbot.zermo.org).

Sign in at auth.zermo.org with the Demo Account listed in App Store Connect. Do not use a LAN gateway token.

After sign-in: pick or spawn a unit, send a short message, confirm it appears in the ledger and a run starts. Swipe Units → Chat → Computer → Settings. Optional: dictation key (microphone prompt). Check Path thinking in the footer while a run is busy.

Sounds and keyboard live on the console; force-quit to refresh. Demo credentials belong only in App Store Connect, never in review notes on GitHub.
